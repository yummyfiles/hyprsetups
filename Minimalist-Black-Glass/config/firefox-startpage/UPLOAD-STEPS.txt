How to upload v1.0.5 to the AMO dev hub
========================================

Files you need:
  - The file to upload:
    /home/yummy.dev/.config/firefox-startpage/startpage.xpi
  - A text editor open, in case the form asks for notes.

Steps
-----
Step 1
  Open your add-on edit page in the browser:
  https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/developers/addon/07fa410c5cd04fa69180/edit

Step 2
  On that page find and click "Manage the versions"
  (or "View versions" / "Versions" in the left menu).

Step 3
  In the versions list click the button "Add a new version".
  It usually sits near the top-right of the list.

Step 4
  A form appears. In the "Upload a file" box, drag and drop
  /home/yummy.dev/.config/firefox-startpage/startpage.xpi

Step 5
  If it asks for source code: you can leave it empty.
  Your add-on is plain HTML/JS, not obfuscated, so no source
  upload is required.

Step 6
  Confirm compatibility is set to Firefox.
  Optionally write a short changelog note, for example:
    "New tab matches the current startpage, adds GitHub stats widget."

Step 7
  Click "Submit to add-on review".

Step 8
  Wait for approval. When approved, install the update in Firefox
  (Firefox usually offers it automatically; otherwise re-open the
  add-on page on AMO and click "Add to Firefox").

Then tell me when it's approved and I will check that the new tab
matches startpage.html exactly.
